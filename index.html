How to use

  1. Find the family. Tap a face along the top, or tap any colored row to open it.
  2. Pick the closest word. Each family has three groups, from mild to intense .
  3. Go deeper. Tap any word to see more specific words, what the feeling may be asking for, and one small thing to try.
  4. Log it if you like. Tap Log this feeling to save it with an optional note, then see patterns in your log.

More than one fits? That's normal. You can feel relieved and sad at the same time. Log each one.

Not sure what you feel? Start with Body at the end of the list. Notice where something shows up physically, then follow its links to the feelings it often goes with.

Search finds any of the 450+ words and takes you to where it lives.

Keep it on your phone. iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

Your log

Your log is saved only on this device. Nobody else can see it, including whoever shared this guide with you. Clearing your browser data erases it, so copy it somewhere if you want to keep it.
